# Marketing drafts

Drafts for docs/MARKETING.md. Nothing here has been posted or sent. Placeholders in `{braces}` are filled from real
data or by the owner; anything marked **OWNER** must come from the owner (their story, their account).

| file | what |
|---|---|
| [press_kit.md](press_kit.md) | fact sheet, boilerplate, screenshots list: becomes `/press.html` once the domain exists |
| [launch_posts.md](launch_posts.md) | Show HN, Product Hunt, and one Reddit post per community |
| [outreach.md](outreach.md) | car club email, founding beta invite, press pitch, creator pitch, follow-up |
| [social.md](social.md) | road of the week format, the first 4 weeks, three short video scripts |
| [pro_copy.md](pro_copy.md) | the Pro sheet, trial and tip jar copy (MONETIZATION.md) |

Every draft follows MARKETING.md section 8: no speeds, no racing, no invented numbers or quotes, the developer
disclosed in community posts, only what the shipped build does.
