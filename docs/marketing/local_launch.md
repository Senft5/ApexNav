## QR card (business card, both sides)

Front: the tulip mark, **VETTA**, "Find the good roads near Cincy."
Back: "A co-driver that calls every corner, and a clip of your best one. Free on iPhone." + QR to the App Store link
with `ct=cards` (TestFlight link during the beta). About $20–25 for 250 at any print shop; optional, ask first.

## Clip caption (Reels, TikTok, Shorts)

```
{Road name}, {county}. {One line on the corner: "the left-hander after the bridge tightens twice."}
Called by Vetta, my co-driver app. Free on iPhone, link in bio.
#cincinnati #drivingroads #{car} #backroads
```
Post only clips at a legal, calm pace; turn the speed readout off for public posts once the clip has that switch.
