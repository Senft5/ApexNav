# Social drafts

## Road of the week: format

One 20–30 s vertical video (1080×1920) a week, rendered by web/flyover_video.js from the map and terrain, posted to
every channel the owner set up (MARKETING.md section 6). Numbers come from the road page, labelled honestly.

| part | text |
|---|---|
| on-screen title (0–3 s) | {road name}, {state} |
| on-screen line (3–20 s) | {length} mi · {corners} corners · {climb} ft of climb (from the map) |
| if 2+ members drove it | Measured: {smooth / some rough patches / rough}, {banked / flat} corners |
| end card (last 3 s) | ApexNav · the co-driver for the long way home |
| caption | {why it's worth it, one sentence}. {length} miles, {corners} corners. Plan it in ApexNav: {campaign link} |
| hashtags (Instagram, TikTok only, 3 max) | #drivingroads #{state}roads #{car community if relevant} |

Never in a clip: a speed, a time, a speedometer, a phone in a hand, traffic, a real person's drive or position.

Between road videos (one post each, mid-week):

- **How it works, in 15 s**: the cockpit calls "left 4, crest, right 3 tightens" over a bundled demo drive.
- **Why no subscription**: one card with the MONETIZATION.md promise list, plainly.
- **Road data, explained**: the phone on the dash, then a road section going from map-only to measured.
- **Convoys**: three cars on one map, a hazard pin relayed down the line (filmed on a real crew drive during the beta: there is no bundled convoy demo).

## Short video scripts

### 1. "Your phone, a co-driver" (15 s)

| time | picture | sound |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | cockpit on a dash mount, demo drive running | co-driver: "Left 4. Crest. Right 3 tightens." |
| 3–9 | the map strip showing the next three corners | "ApexNav reads the road ahead out loud." |
| 9–13 | Log: the drive's corners, a smooth score | "And scores how smoothly you drove it." |
| 13–15 | end card | "Never how fast." |

### 2. "The road, measured" (20 s)

| time | picture | sound |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | a road on the map, plain line | "Maps know a road's shape." |
| 5–12 | the same road coloured by measured feel (coverage map) | "Your phone feels the rest: banking, crests, the rough bits." |
| 12–17 | loop builder picking the smooth road over the rough one | "So the good roads rise to the top." |
| 17–20 | end card | "Free. No ads. No subscription." |

| time | picture | sound |
