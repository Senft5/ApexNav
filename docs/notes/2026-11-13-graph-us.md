## 2026-11-13 Our road graph for the lower 48: the build and the hosting (claude/project-thread-39xbx9)
- Big bet #2 (docs/notes/2026-11-13-big-bets.md): our router (fun scored into every edge, works offline) covers OH/KY/IN
  only; elsewhere Fun, Long way and loops fail honestly. This PR is the pipeline for the whole lower 48.
- R2 is not enabled on the account (the API says "Please enable R2 through the Cloudflare Dashboard": needs the owner's
  card, bet #1), and ~4 GB a build would bloat the graph-data branch on every rebuild. So: GitHub releases, free for a
  public repo and outside git history.
- `tools/graph_etl/us_plan.py`: Geofabrik's index -> the lower-48 states and their boxes; one job per 1 degree band x
  15 degree chunk (no job past 6 h: Ohio's densest ten blocks took 105 min), blocks only where a state touches, each job
  fetching the states under its blocks' 0.4 degree margins. `--only` makes a pilot.
- `tools/graph_etl/pack.py`: tiles into 2 degree packs (a release takes 1000 files; the token about 1000 requests an
  hour), packs.json (tile -> pack, offset, length) and the phone's slim index.json.
- `.github/workflows/graph-us.yml`: plan -> osm (each state downloaded once, an artifact) -> band (graph-region.yml's
  per-block build; a failed block keeps the rest and fails the job for a re-run) -> publish (merge, pack, release, then
  the graph-data pointer `us/v1/current.json`; the two newest US releases kept).
- `backend/src/graph.ts`: with a pointer, the index and packs from the release (memoized per isolate an hour) and each
  tile as a Range of its pack (a 200 without Range: cut from the whole pack); bad tag, missing files or packs of another
  build: the branch's tiles as before. No pointer is asked again only hourly.
- Tests: `test_graph_us.py` (plan, packs byte for byte, the workflow's order), `backend/test/graph.test.ts` (release
  tiles by Range, without Range, the fallbacks).
- Next: the West Virginia pilot run (this branch's push of tools/graph_etl/US_BUILD starts it); check its release's
  packs against its tiles, then the full lower 48 (`only=` empty) and the app's "covers Ohio, Kentucky and Indiana so
  far" lines once it is live.
