## 2026-11-14: One bar that carries nothing counts as no signal
- Done: the kept copies a saved route carries (members' reports, members' road feel) used to stand in only when the phone said it was offline (`navigator.onLine === false`). On a mountain road the phone often says it's online with one bar that carries nothing, so the card and the drive got nothing at all. Now they also stand in when the live ask doesn't get through:
  - The route card's reports line asks with an 8 s limit and falls back to the kept reports, with their age.
  - The road feel falls back to the kept copy when membership can't be asked (`ApexSync.member()` answers null on a failed call; the user is still signed in on the phone) or the members' data can't be fetched. The card's "(kept …)" now follows the copy itself.
  - A drive's reports ahead fill every cell the live ask missed from the kept ones.
  - `hazardsAround` no longer keeps a failed ask as the area's answer for 5 minutes: the next plan asks again. An HTTP answer, even a refusal, still counts as an answer.
