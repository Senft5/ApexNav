## Session 2026-11-13 (big bets): the 12 biggest gaps against the best apps in each pillar (claude/project-thread-539ta5)

Nate (2026-11-13): threads are sliding into copy and spacing tweaks; he wants major improvements. This is the ranked list
of what would change why someone picks Vetta, from the repo, IDEAS, OWNER_TODO and a run of the app (tools/app_tour.js
stills). Polish is left out on purpose. Compared against: Kurviger, Calimoto, Scenic, REVER (fun routing); Apple Maps,
Google Maps, Waze (maps UI); Strava, Garmin Catalyst, Harry's LapTimer, RaceChrono (analysis); Strava, Instagram, Waze
(social). Sizes use IDEAS' scale (S one PR, M a few, L many across core/web/native, XL a project). "Owner" = blocked on
Nate's money, account or phone.

| # | Big bet | What a driver gets (the gap today) | Size | Thread |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **A backend that survives launch**: Workers Paid, R2, no public demo servers on the hot path | Today about 500 km of road uploads a day trips the Free plan's D1 cap and takes the whole app down until midnight UTC, sign-in included (2026-11-13-free-plan-caps). Fun routing, the fallback and Overpass lean on free public servers with no uptime promise. Apple and Google never go down; we can't either. | M + owner ($5/mo, R2 card) | production |
| 2 | **Fun routing everywhere in the US**: our own pre-scored graph beyond OH/KY/IN | Our router (fun scored into every edge, works offline) covers 3 states; everywhere else Fun and Long way depend on the public Valhalla demo server and, by Nate's rule, fail honestly when it does. Kurviger and Calimoto work in every country. Build the lower 48 in the graph-region CI (more shards), serve from R2. | L (+ R2 from #1) | routing |
| 3 | **Push notifications** | Duels, a friend passing your time attack, crew drive RSVPs, friend requests and kudos sit in For you until you happen to open the app. Strava, Instagram and Waze run on push. APNs key (owner), a Worker sender, ask at the moment of need, silent while driving (C-06). Check first that the Worker can speak HTTP/2 to APNs; if not, a free relay. | M + owner (APNs key) | social |
| 4 | **Drive posts and a friends feed** | Strava's core loop is drive → post → kudos and comments. Vetta has duels, crews, roadbooks and time attacks, but no place where a friend's drive shows up. After a drive, one tap posts a card (map with the private ends cut, Score, best corners, photos, roads stamped) to friends or a crew; a feed with kudos and comments. | L (migration) | social |
| 5 | **Synced video and shareable corner clips** | Catalyst's killer feature is video synced to the analysis; Instagram's is the clip. Our dashcam overlay needs a separate recording and a hand-set offset. Record from the mount during the drive, auto-synced, cut each great corner into a vertical clip with the g trace and call burned in, one tap to Reels or a post (#4). Growth and analysis in one. | L | analysis + social |
| 6 | **Discover: a public library of great roads on the web** | REVER, Calimoto and Scenic sell on a browsable library of routes. Road pages exist in the app; put one public page per great road in the US (map, archetype, surface, best direction, Drive it in Vetta), shareable and searchable. Every share and search lands on Vetta. Needs the domain (owner). | M | routing |
| 7 | **CarPlay ready on entitlement day** | Every rival in the car is on CarPlay. The map, the next note and hazard alerts as CPAlert (C-01, C-02) should be built and simulator-tested now so the entitlement is a switch, not a project. | L, owner (entitlement) | CarPlay |
| 8 | **Real inputs: OBD-II and 10–25 Hz GNSS** | Catalyst, Harry's and RaceChrono read the car and a fast GPS; we infer throttle and brake from the phone at 1 Hz GPS. A $20–40 BLE adapter gives true throttle, brake, RPM and gear for the deepest analysis on the market (D-12, D-13). | L + owner (~$40 hardware) | analysis |
| 9 | **Proof on real roads** | The analysis is only as good as its ground truth. Nate's real drives become regression fixtures; road feel is checked against surfaces we know; one published hour-of-driving battery figure; app-open sharing checked on a phone (Q-21, P-01). Catalyst wins on trust. | M + owner (drives) | analysis |
| 10 | **Native-speed app** | The map page loads up to ~145 scripts and 3.6 MB before it is ready (startup budget). Apple and Google Maps are instant. A build step (bundle, minify, cache) and a cold start under 1 s (P-06) is felt on every launch. | M | UI |
| 11 | **Plan on the laptop, drive it from the phone** | Kurviger and Scenic win on the desk: plan a day trip on a big screen and it is waiting in the car. Our desk layout exists (A-05), but saved routes and places live only on the device that made them. Sync them to your account so a route planned anywhere opens in Where to? on the phone. | M (migration) | routing |
| 12 | **Android** | About 4 in 10 US drivers can't install Vetta, and their friends can't join a crew. The web cockpit already runs on Android; a WebView shell with native sensors (A-01) is the cheapest way in. Owner call: Android was "low priority" (2026-10-25). | XL, owner | production |
