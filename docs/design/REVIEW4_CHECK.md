## 3. Verdict

Yes, it is better to live with, and not by a little. The first recording showed a co-driver that spoke once in seven
minutes; this one shows one that reads the road: "Tight right, careful" seven seconds out at 64 mph, "Very sharp left,
almost back on itself, long, careful" with the hook on the car's card, "Quiet from here" when the road straightens and
the notes back when it does not, the road under the car named on the strip, a time on the trip, the car in every drive
from the first second to the idle map after End, the welcome a new driver was never shown now the first thing they see,
and the Log, reached at last with a scored drive, is the best page in the app. Twenty-six of thirty-two findings are
simply fixed, which is a rate I did not expect overnight. It feels closer to the app it wants to be because the
promise, a co-driver, is now audible; the first recording only had the furniture.

But the drive also went somewhere the first never did, and there it broke worse than anything the first found. Where
the route doubles back on its own road the co-driver said "Back on route" a hundred and forty times in half a minute,
then "Wrong way" for seven minutes while the car was on the route and the road's best corners went by unread. That is
the single thing to fix before anyone outside the owner drives it, because every loop and every out-and-back is that
road. The next three things between it and greatness: the co-driver must be right when the route is strange (U-90,
then U-96 and U-93, so it never calls a corner from inside it and never says metres to a driver in miles); the numbers
must agree with each other (the end card and the Log give different scores for the same drive, U-91; the sight plate
still flickers where there is no limit, U-60; a forecast that is twenty minutes optimistic, U-97; a map behind the end
card that is never the drive, U-88); and the biggest words on the screen must never be cut where the meaning is (U-92,
with the smaller cuts in U-99, U-101, U-105). None of those is a redesign either. The one thing I would do today,
before any of them, is U-94: the recording is posting gravel onto a real road.
