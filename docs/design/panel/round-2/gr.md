# Grace Okonkwo (GR), round 2

Haiku. 67, retired teacher, Sunday drives with my husband in our Buick. Evidence: recording 7 (`scratchpad/r7`), with
the photos I looked at cited by flow and file.

## First impressions

I can read the first screen. "Your co-driver for the long way home" is big and plain, and I like it. Then the
welcome shows me "CREST", "OPEN L", "TIGHT L", "LONG" in small grey caps (`first-run/phone/002-welcome-1.png`). I do
not know what those words mean, and nothing tells me. "Watch a replay" is a small underlined line at the bottom
(same photo). A replay would teach me more than the six looping slides, and it is the hardest thing on the screen to
find.

The map is calm. The "Where to?" box and the Start key are clear (`first-run/phone/009-idle-map.png`). The suggested
road, "Mount Hope Road", is useful. Its "Drive" and "Plan" are small underlined words, not buttons, and I would miss
them with my thumb.

The driving screen is the one I trust. The speed is big, the tab bar is gone, and Report and End are plain boxes
(`free-drive/phone/003-driving.png`). Then a red "55 Sight" box appears (`free-drive/phone/011-driving.png`). The word
"Sight" is in grey, and I would not know that a red number means slow down. The drive is safe only if I learn that
word first.

The Layers panel has eight switches. The only sign of on or off is the colour of the square
(`first-run/phone/010-layers.png`). The route card is full of numbers that need a teacher: "TWIST 56", "FLOW 23",
"SURFACE --" (`planning/phone/004-route-card.png`). The text is grey and small, and "Save / Share / GPX / Pin place"
are tiny links at the bottom of the card.

## Proposals

**GR-1. Put a plain sentence on the red warning.** When the Sight number is under your speed, the binnacle says in
words: "Slow to 55 for what you can see." Build: replace the bare "Sight" chip with a short sentence that shows for
3 seconds on each change, and keep the number. Done looks like: a driver who has never read the app can say what
the red number means at a glance. Cost: S.

**GR-2. Bigger text and bigger keys, with a floor.** Body and caption text at least 17 px on a 390-pt phone; every
link that acts becomes a button with a 44-pt target. Also fix the two numbers in `contrast.md` that fail
outright: the Tapedeck dial "60" (1:1) and the Roadbook "MPH" label (4.22:1). Done looks like: at the largest
system text size the route card and the Garage fit without clipping, and no label on the driving screen is under
4.5:1. Cost: M.

**GR-3. Switches that say on or off, and a route card with one "More" row.** Each Layers switch shows the word "On"
or "Off". On the route card, Save, Share, GPX and Pin place move under one "More" row, and only Drive it and Preview
stay at full size. Done looks like: I can tell from the page what is on, and I see two big choices on the card. Cost: S.

**GR-4. A real replay on the welcome.** "Watch a replay" becomes a second button under Start, and the replay shows
the call and the red warning in use. Done looks like: a new driver watches one corner called before she taps Start.
Cost: S.

## Did round 1 land?

Yes for the call grammar. The recording's transcript reads "Careful, tight right", "Hairpin left, long" and "Quiet
from here. Bumps and cameras only." Warning first, in plain words. Yes for the road suggestion: the idle map names a
road and gives a reason ("Not driven yet", "11 good corners"). I could not confirm the Undo on End from the photos,
so I say nothing about it.

## What I'd protect

1. The driving screen with no tab bar, big speed and the two plain boxes Report and End.
2. The call words in order, with the warning first.
