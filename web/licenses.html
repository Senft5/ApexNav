The open software, data and voices ApexNav uses, with the notices their licences ask us to ship. Kept in step with THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md (IDEAS L-03).

Shipped in the app

  • MapLibre GL JS 4.7.1: map rendering. BSD 3-Clause. Copyright (c) MapLibre contributors; portions copyright (c) Mapbox, Inc.
  • maplibre-contour: contour lines on the map. BSD 3-Clause. Copyright (c) Michael Barry and contributors.
  • QR Code Generator for JavaScript 2.0.4: the convoy QR code. MIT. Copyright (c) 2009 Kazuhiko Arase. "QR Code" is a registered trademark of DENSO WAVE INCORPORATED.
  • Kokoro-82M (hexgrad) and its voice packs: the recorded co-driver voices. Apache License 2.0.
  • misaki 0.9.4 (hexgrad) dictionaries and graphemes_to_phonemes (Peter Reid) models: words to sounds. Apache License 2.0.
  • Fonts: Barlow Condensed, Inter Tight, IBM Plex. SIL Open Font License 1.1.

Data and services

  • Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License (ODbL 1.0), served by OpenFreeMap.
  • Routing, map matching and posted limits: Valhalla (MIT) on the FOSSGIS public server.
  • Address search: Nominatim (OpenStreetMap data, ODbL). Search suggestions: Photon by Komoot (OpenStreetMap data, ODbL).
  • Elevation: AWS Terrain Tiles (Terrarium), from the sources listed at registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles.
  • Plate-reading cameras: OpenStreetMap (surveillance:type=ALPR, DeFlock), ODbL.

Website only

The website can speak road names with transformers.js (Apache License 2.0, bundling onnxruntime-web, MIT), downloaded when that switch is turned on. The iOS app never loads it.
