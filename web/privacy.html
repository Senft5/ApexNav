Privacy

ApexNav is built so that how you drive stays yours. Most of what the app knows never leaves your phone. This page lists everything that does, where it goes, and how to remove it.

Last updated 28 October 2026

On your phone only

  • Your drives: position, speed, g-forces, banking, bumps and roughness every 5 m. Saved in the app's storage on the phone. They are never uploaded unless you share one yourself.
  • Privacy zones you set (home, work). Inside one, nothing is recorded or shared and convoys don't see you.
  • Spots you save, until you share one.
  • Crash check contact: the name and number you enter. Used only to open a text to them, which you send, after a hard impact. Never uploaded.
  • Voice: notes are spoken by a voice model that runs on the phone. No audio is recorded or sent.
  • Settings and your route history.

If you make an account

Signing in is optional. The cockpit, routing, recording and the Log all work without an account.

  • From Google sign-in: your Google account id, email address and display name. Your display name is shown only to you and to friends you accept; everyone else sees your handle.
  • What you add: your handle, friends, blocks, crews, garage cars, shared spots and ratings, results you choose to share (on a stage: your best Driver Score and the date of each day you drove it, so friends can see who drove it most this month; times only if you share times), and reports you file.
  • Sign-in sessions, which expire after 30 days.

Road data (only if you turn it on)

With Road data switched on, the app helps map how roads feel. What is sent is the map's road id, the direction, a 5 m section number on that road, and four road measurements for it (roughness, banking, crest and bumps). Never coordinates, speeds or times. A random 0.5 to 1.5 km at each end of every trip is never sent, nor anything inside a privacy zone, nor residential and service roads. Once merged into a road's averages, road data is anonymous and is not deleted with your account. A road's averages are shown to other members only once at least two different people have driven it that way.

Convoys

In a convoy you joined, the other cars see your live position, heading and handle while the convoy runs. Positions are kept in memory only and are gone when the convoy ends. Nothing is sent from inside a privacy zone.

Shared arrival times

When you share your ETA, anyone with the link sees your handle and when you'll arrive, refreshed about once a minute while you drive. Never where you are, your route or where you're going. It ends when you arrive or stop it, or after 4 hours, and is deleted a day later.

Crew drives

When you plan a drive for a crew, its name, time, meeting point and notes are seen by that crew's members, with who planned it and who's going (by handle). A public crew can list a drive as a meet: then members within about 80 km also see its name, time, meeting point and how many are going, never who. It is deleted a week after it starts, or with your account.

Shared routes

When you share a planned route, its shape (without the first 400 m) and name go to the friends and crews you pick, for 30 days or until you delete it. Never a recorded drive.

Hazard reports

When you report a hazard (pothole, gravel, police…), its kind, the spot where you were and the direction you were travelling are shown to other members nearby until it expires (30 minutes to 7 days by kind). They never see who reported it. "Still there?" answers are counted the same way.

App health counts (switch in Settings → Your data)

Once a day the app sends yesterday's totals of a few events, such as routes found or failed, road uploads that failed and how long the phone mount took to set, so problems get fixed. No account, phone, position or time of day is in them, and no session is sent with them. Totals are kept 90 days. Turn it off and nothing is counted or sent.

Feedback (Settings → Feedback)

What you write is sent to the person who makes ApexNav, with, if you leave the box ticked, the app's version, the kind of device, screen size, language and how many errors the app saw since you opened it. Never your position, drives or contacts. Your handle is sent only if you are signed in and ask for a reply. Feedback is kept 180 days.

Crash reports

If the app hits an error, it sends the error message and where in the app it happened, with numbers, emails and links removed. Nothing in it identifies you. Daily counts are kept for 30 days.

Services the app talks to

To draw maps and plan routes the app asks these services directly. They see your phone's internet address and what is asked of them:

  • Map tiles: OpenFreeMap (map area on screen). Terrain: Amazon Web Services public terrain tiles.
  • Routing and map matching: the public Valhalla server (openstreetmap.de) and OSRM. They receive the start, end and stops of a route you plan, and, when the Log matches a drive to roads, that drive's track with its first and last km removed.
  • Search: Photon (komoot) and Nominatim (OpenStreetMap) for what you type in Where to?
  • Road names and features: Overpass (OpenStreetMap).
  • Car lookup: NHTSA's vehicle database (US Department of Transportation), only when you look a car up in the Garage. It receives the VIN or the make and year you typed.
  • Fonts: Google Fonts.
  • Our server runs on Cloudflare, which processes requests to it.

ApexNav has no advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, and sells no data.

Keeping and deleting

  • Download my data (Account) gives you everything the server holds about you as a file.
  • Delete account (Account) erases your account, friends, crews you own, shared results and spots, and removes you from what other people hold. Server backups are kept for up to 8 weeks, so a deleted account can remain in a backup copy until then.
  • Deleting the app removes everything stored on the phone.
