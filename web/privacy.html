Privacy

Vetta is built so that how you drive stays yours. Most of what the app knows never leaves your phone. This page lists everything that does, where it goes, and how to remove it.

Last updated 30 October 2026

On your phone only

  • Your drives: position, speed, g-forces, banking, bumps and roughness every 5 m. Saved in the app's storage on the phone. They are never uploaded unless you share one yourself.
  • Privacy zones (home zones) you set (home, work). Inside one, nothing is recorded or shared and convoys don't see you. They stay on the phone, except while you show yourself on the map to friends or crews: then a copy goes to our server so it can refuse a position inside one too, and it's deleted when you choose Nobody.
  • Spots you save, until you share one. A photo you add to a spot is redrawn without its location, time and camera details and never leaves the phone, even when you share the spot.
  • Pinned places (Home, Work and places you name) and recent destinations. Never uploaded.
  • Your notes from a recce drive and the calls you changed. Never uploaded.
  • Crash check contact: the name and number you enter. Used only to open a text to them, which you send, after a hard impact. Never uploaded.
  • Voice: notes are spoken by a voice model that runs on the phone. No audio is recorded or sent.
  • Settings and your route history.

If you make an account

Signing in is optional. The cockpit, routing, recording and the Log all work without an account.

  • From Google sign-in: your Google account id, email address and display name. Your display name is shown only to you and to friends you accept; everyone else sees your handle.
  • What you add: your handle, friends, blocks, crews, garage cars, shared spots and ratings, messages you write in a crew's chat (seen by that crew's members, deleted after 30 days), your position on the friends map only if you choose who sees you (sent while you drive, held back by the delay you pick, snapped to a one-mile grid unless you choose Exact, kept no longer than the delay needs and deleted when you choose Nobody), a build thread for a car (its steps, parts, photos and the Vetta Score of a drive you pick for a step; seen by whoever sees that car) and replies you write on others', photos you add (a profile photo, your cars' photos; redrawn on the phone before upload, which drops where and when they were taken and the camera details, and checked again on our server; seen only by signed-in members, and a report hides one at once while it's looked at; removing one deletes it), results you choose to share (on a stage: your best Vetta Score and the date of each day you drove it, so friends can see who drove it most this month; times only if you share times; to a crew you share Scores with, each drive's Vetta Score and its date, never where or how fast, added up on the crew's Score board and deleted when you stop sharing, leave the crew or close your account), and reports you file. Kudos you give (a tap, no text) are seen by the person who drove; kudos are kept 90 days. An accepted friend can open your profile, which shows only what you already share with them: your name, cars you show friends, crews you share, and stages whose scores you share with them.
  • Sign-in sessions, which expire after 30 days.

Road data (only if you turn it on)

With Road data switched on, the app helps map how roads feel. What is sent is the map's road id, the direction, a 5 m section number on that road, and four road measurements for it (roughness, banking, crest and bumps), plus that road's own line and kind (a highway, a back road) as drawn on the public map (OpenStreetMap), so the coverage map can show which roads are measured. Never your position, your path, speeds or times. A random 0.5 to 1.5 km at each end of every trip is never sent, nor anything inside a privacy zone, nor residential and service roads. Once merged into a road's averages, road data is anonymous and is not deleted with your account. A through road's averages (highways, main and secondary roads, and long back roads) are shown to other members from the first drive, marked as less sure; any other road only once at least two different people have driven it that way, so a lane nobody else drives never shows yours. Members see how sure a road's numbers are, never how many people or drives measured it. Your account keeps how many km of road data you shared each month, and how many you were first to map, for your challenges; your crews see only the total of all their members. It is deleted with your account.

Convoys

In a convoy you joined, the other cars see your live position, heading and handle while the convoy runs. Positions are kept in memory only and are gone when the convoy ends. Nothing is sent from inside a privacy zone.

Shared arrival times

When you share your ETA, anyone with the link sees your handle and when you'll arrive, refreshed about once a minute while you drive. Never where you are, your route or where you're going. It ends when you arrive or stop it, or after 4 hours, and is deleted a day later.

Crew drives

When you plan a drive for a crew, its name, time, meeting point and notes are seen by that crew's members, with who planned it and who's going (by handle). A public crew can list a drive as a meet: then members within about 80 km also see its name, time, meeting point and how many are going, never who. When you start or join a drive's convoy from its card, or say you were there afterwards, the crew's members see that you came (by handle, never where you were), and the drive shows the best Vetta Score a crew-mate who came had already shared with the crew that day. It is all deleted a week after the drive starts, or with your account.

Shared routes

When you share a planned route, its shape (without the first 400 m) and name go to the friends and crews you pick, for 30 days or until you delete it. Never a recorded drive.

Hazard reports

When you report a hazard (pothole, gravel, police…), its kind, the spot where you were and the direction you were travelling are shown to other members nearby until it expires (30 minutes to 7 days by kind). They never see who reported it. "Still there?" answers are counted the same way.

App health counts (switch in Garage → Your data)

Once a day the app sends yesterday's totals of a few events, such as routes found or failed, road uploads that failed and how long the phone mount took to set, so problems get fixed. No account, phone, position or time of day is in them, and no session is sent with them. Totals are kept 90 days. Turn it off and nothing is counted or sent.

The app also asks the server, a few times a day, which of its features are switched on. With that it sends the app's version and a random number this phone made up, used only to pick a share of phones when a feature is tried out on some first. The server doesn't keep either.

Feedback (Garage → Feedback)

What you write is sent to the person who makes Vetta, with, if you leave the box ticked, the app's version, the kind of device, screen size, language and how many errors the app saw since you opened it. Never your position, drives or contacts. Your handle is sent only if you are signed in and ask for a reply. Feedback is kept 180 days.

Crash reports

If the app hits an error, it sends the error message and where in the app it happened, with numbers, emails and links removed. Nothing in it identifies you. Daily counts are kept for 30 days.

Services the app talks to

To draw maps and plan routes the app asks these services directly. They see your phone's internet address and what is asked of them:

  • Map tiles: OpenFreeMap (map area on screen). Terrain: Amazon Web Services public terrain tiles.
  • Routing and map matching: the public Valhalla server (openstreetmap.de) and OSRM. They receive the start, end and stops of a route you plan, and, when the Log matches a drive to roads, that drive's track with its first and last km removed.
  • Search: Photon (komoot) and Nominatim (OpenStreetMap) for what you type in Where to?
  • Road names and features: Overpass (OpenStreetMap).
  • Car lookup: NHTSA's vehicle database (US Department of Transportation), only when you look a car up in the Garage. It receives the VIN or the make and year you typed.
  • Weather: Open-Meteo, for the forecast along a route you plan. It receives six points along the route.
  • Our server runs on Cloudflare, which processes requests to it.

Vetta has no advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, and sells no data.

Keeping and deleting

  • Download my data (Account) gives you everything the server holds about you as a file.
  • Delete account (Account) erases your account, friends, crews you own, shared results and spots, and removes you from what other people hold. Server backups are kept for up to 8 weeks, so a deleted account can remain in a backup copy until then.
  • Deleting the app removes everything stored on the phone.
