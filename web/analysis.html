How Score works

Score rates how you drive the corners, not how fast. Pace: corner speed against its safe grip limit, full credit from 85 % to 100 %. Smooth: how gently cornering load builds. Brakes: no hard braking after the apex. Rhythm: even commitment from corner to corner. Speed limits never enter it. 1000 is a closed-course number, not something to chase on public roads.
