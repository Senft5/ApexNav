Driver Score (0-1000) rates technique, never time or speed: pace = corner minimum speed as a share of its sane grip limit (full credit from 85 % to 100 %, falling steeply beyond — overdriving never scores), smoothness = how gently lateral load builds per metre, braking = no hard braking after the apex, consistency = even commitment corner to corner. Speed limits never enter it. A perfect score means driving right at each corner's limit — a closed-track figure, not something to chase on public roads.
