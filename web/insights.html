Road

Tap a road to see its figures.

Rules every figure passes
  • Facts about road sections only. Never a drive, a path, a time of day or a person.
  • At least 10 different drivers on that road and direction.
  • Through roads only, never residential streets or lanes.
  • By month, with noise added so no one driver can be picked out.
Endpoints
GET /api/data/v1/meta
GET /api/data/v1/roads.geojson?bbox=s,w,n,e
GET /insights-sample.geojson

Real data needs a key. The box is at most 1° a side. The sample has the same fields with made-up values.

Example
curl -H "Authorization: Bearer vk_…" \
  "https://data.vettadrive.com/api/data/v1/roads.geojson?bbox=39.2,-84.4,39.3,-84.25"
Fields
API key

Paste a key to see real measured roads in view. It stays in this tab only.
