Drive within the law

Vetta helps you find and enjoy good roads. It is not a racing app. Obey speed limits, traffic laws and road conditions at all times. Pace notes, corner speeds, scores and routes are aids, can be wrong, and never replace your own judgement. Don't touch the phone while driving; set it up before you go or let a passenger use it.

Your account

You are responsible for what happens under your account. Keep one account per person. You can delete it at any time in the app.

What you share

You keep ownership of what you post (handles, crew names, spots, ratings, car details, shared results). You allow Vetta to show it to the people you share it with and to run the service.

There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Don't post anything hateful, harassing, sexual, violent, illegal, or that reveals someone else's private information, and don't use the app to organise street racing or other unlawful driving. Anyone can block a person and report content from wherever it appears. Reported content is hidden after several independent reports and reviewed within 24 hours; content that breaks these terms is removed and the account responsible is banned.

Road data

If you turn on Road data, you allow Vetta to keep the anonymous road measurements you contribute as part of the shared road map, also after you delete your account (see Privacy).

The service

Changes
