Privacy zones

Places like home and work. Inside one, nothing is recorded or shared, and convoys don't see you. Kept on this phone only.

Crash check

After a hard impact and a stop, Vetta asks if you're OK. If you don't answer within a minute it shows buttons to call emergency services or text this person your location. Your phone never calls or texts by itself. This is a backup to your phone's own crash detection, not a replacement for it. The contact stays on this phone.

Units

Driving side: bends toward your own side of the road get more caution, since you see less round them.

Feedback

Something wrong, or an idea? It goes straight to the person who makes Vetta.

About Vetta

Version--
  • No races. No speed or time leaderboards, and no record of how fast anyone went.
  • Yours until you share it. Drives stay on this phone; privacy zones keep home and work out of everything.
  • Unknown stays unknown. When the app doesn't know, it shows --, never a guess.
  • Free to drive. No ads, no personal data sold, and nothing you made ever behind a paywall.
