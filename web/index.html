Destination · spirited routing

Loop from here

Loops are stitched from the best roads nearby: every road around you is scored from map geometry (corners per km, few junctions), the best stretches are chained to fit the distance, and the result is checked against a plain loop. Geometry only until driven.

Highlights · scenic stops

When on, loops lean toward roads that pass a highlight you rate 3 stars or more. They can tip the choice between two good roads; they never make a dull road win. Yours stay on this phone until accounts are connected.
