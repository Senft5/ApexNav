01 · Find

Finds the good roads.

Pick a length. Vetta builds a loop from the twistiest roads around you and brings you home.

  • 15 to 90 mile loops
  • Fun route to anywhere
  • Calls work with no signal

02 · Score

Quick and clean.

Every drive gets a Vetta Score, corner by corner: how much of each corner's grip you used, and how smoothly. On a road you've driven, the co-driver calls the gap to your best, live.

  • Corner by corner
  • Live gaps to your best
  • Replay any drive

03 · Share

Post the drive.

A card with the road's outline and your score, and a clip of your best corner filmed from the mount.

  • One-tap share card
  • Best-corner clips
  • Your friends' feed

04 · Crew

Bring the crew.

Plan the Sunday drive, stay together on one map, and see who's quickest on the crew's roads.

  • Crews and convoys
  • Stage times
  • Time attack boards
